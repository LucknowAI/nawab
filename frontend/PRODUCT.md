# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Local residents of Uttar Pradesh cities (Lucknow, Kanpur, Varanasi, Noida) seeking practical city info: metro fares and routes, civic services, local Q&A, often bilingual (Hindi/English). Varying tech comfort and literacy levels. Secondary audience: UP AI Labs community members exploring the assistant as an AI capability demo.

## Product Purpose

Nawab AI is a conversational copilot that answers everyday city-life questions per selected city, each city carrying its own accent color and identity (Lucknow gold, Varanasi orange, Kanpur blue, Noida purple). Built by UP AI Labs, a Lucknow-founded nonprofit igniting AI knowledge-sharing across UP. Success: a resident gets a fast, trustworthy answer without needing technical fluency.

## Brand Personality

Warm heritage, dignified. Nawabi courtly elegance (gold, ivory/parchment, ink) rather than sterile tech minimalism. Unhurried, trustworthy, respectful tone; architectural motifs (e.g. Rumi Darwaza) signal place and heritage rather than decoration for its own sake.

## Anti-references

Do not look like a generic SaaS/AI chatbot: no flat dark mode, no purple gradients, no robotic tech-bro aesthetic, no glassmorphism/neon. Do not look like a dry bureaucratic government portal either. The heritage palette and motifs already in the codebase are the anchor; new UI must extend that identity, not default to chatbot-template conventions.

## Design Principles

- Heritage carries identity: color, type, and motif choices should read as "Nawabi," never generic-AI-default.
- Plain language over jargon: civic tool for general public, not developers.
- City as a first-class variable: every surface should respect the active city's accent token, not hardcode Lucknow gold.
- Calm confidence over hype: warmth and dignity, not urgency or gamification.
- Bilingual-ready: copy and layout should tolerate Hindi/English mixing without breaking.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA minimum. Plain-language UI copy for a broad literacy range. Support bilingual (Hindi/English) content without layout breakage. Respect reduced-motion preferences given animation use (framer-motion, animejs).
